Inside nfluence · Our own brand

A clearer website. A connected creator workspace.

Our own website brings agency services and creator campaigns together, with a clear next step for each audience.

The brief

Help businesses understand the services available, make project inquiries more focused, and give brands and creators a shared place to manage campaign work.

What we built

  • A clear service offering. Twelve dedicated service pages explain the scope and connect visitors to the relevant project inquiry.
  • Responsive branding. Consistent logo spacing, centered rows and readable content adapt across phone, tablet and desktop layouts.
  • A more useful inquiry path. Service selections carry into the contact form. Clear field labels and delivery feedback help visitors complete their inquiry.
  • A private campaign workspace. Brands and creators can manage applications, conversations, scheduled calls, deliverable reviews and completed payment records.
